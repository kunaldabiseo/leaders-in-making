Honest answers

Things you might be wondering.

If your question isn't here, write to info@leadersinmaking.com — a senior counsellor will reply within one working day.
How is this different from a typical career counselling session?
Most career counselling stops at an aptitude test and a list. We start there — and then sit with the actual decisions: which college, which stream, which job, which conversation with your parents. The work is closer to coaching than counselling.
Do you only work with students, or also adults?
Both. Roughly half our practice is corporate coaching — executives, mid-career professionals returning to work, founders. The other half is split between students, parents and institutional programs.
What does an engagement typically cost?
It depends on scope. A single diagnostic session is priced very differently from a 12-session executive engagement or an institutional partnership. We share specific numbers after the free discovery call — once we know what we're solving.
Do you offer sessions online?
Yes — most of our 1:1 work runs over secure video. In-person sessions are available in New Delhi by appointment, and on-campus engagements for schools and corporates.
I'm certifying my school's counsellors — how does that work?
We have two flagship certifications — Career Counselling and TPO — that we deliver in cohorts. Schools and colleges typically enrol 3–10 faculty members per cycle. We can also build a custom version for your institution.
How quickly will you respond to my form?
Within one working day — usually the same evening. A senior counsellor from the team reviews every form personally. We don't pass leads to a call centre.
